# Feeding Guidelines

Guidance for scheduling meals. Every rule has a stable ID so a generated plan can cite
the rule it relied on.

## FEED-001: Adult dogs eat twice daily, roughly 10-12 hours apart
Most adult dogs do best on two meals a day. A single daily meal increases the risk of
bile vomiting and of gorging. Typical slots are morning (07:00-09:00) and evening
(17:00-19:00).

## FEED-002: Puppies under 6 months eat three to four times daily
Puppies cannot hold enough food in one sitting to meet their energy needs and are prone
to low blood sugar. Schedule 3-4 smaller meals spread across waking hours.

## FEED-003: Adult cats prefer several small meals
Cats are natural grazers. Two to four small meals per day suits most adult cats better
than one large meal. Free-feeding dry food is acceptable for cats at a healthy weight
but not for cats on a weight-management plan.

## FEED-004: Do not exercise a dog within 1 hour of a large meal
Vigorous activity close to a full meal raises the risk of gastric dilatation-volvulus
(bloat), especially in deep-chested breeds. Leave at least 60 minutes between a meal and
a walk or play session, in either direction.

## FEED-005: Deep-chested breeds need stricter meal-exercise separation
Great Danes, Standard Poodles, Weimaraners, Boxers, Setters, and other deep-chested
breeds are at elevated bloat risk. For these breeds extend the meal-to-exercise gap to
2 hours and split the daily ration into smaller portions.

## FEED-006: Keep meal times consistent day to day
Pets regulate digestion and elimination around a predictable feeding schedule. Meal
times that drift more than about an hour between days cause loose stools and begging.

## FEED-007: Fresh water is always available and is not a scheduled task
Water should be available at all times and refreshed at least once daily. Do not model
water as a timed task that competes for a slot; model it as a daily refresh.

## FEED-008: Senior pets benefit from smaller, more frequent meals
Pets over roughly 8 years old (7 for large breeds) often digest smaller portions better.
Consider splitting the same daily ration across three meals instead of two.
